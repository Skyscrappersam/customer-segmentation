How to read the Income vs Spending chart

Each bubble represents a customer. The horizontal position represents annual income, while the vertical position represents total spending.

  • Bubble size represents purchase frequency.
  • Bubble colour represents customer satisfaction.
  • The dashed vertical line represents median annual income.
  • The dashed horizontal line represents median total spending.
  • Hover over a bubble to see the customer's ID, category, income, spending, purchase frequency and satisfaction.
